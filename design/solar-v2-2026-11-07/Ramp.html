Solar, redrawn from the sun

Solar moves to the sun as solar telescopes show it (NASA's 304 Å view and the warm "solar" colormaps built to match): deep red, red-orange, orange, gold, white-hot. Warm colors only, so it no longer shares Plasma's violet and pink. Two takes below, next to today's Solar and the other three schemes. The parchment tone is sampled from the Eastern Kingdoms screenshot; the heat uses Map.lua's fade (alpha 0.02 + 0.6 × v, quiet edge).

All four side by side

A: Sun. The full telescope ramp: oxblood edge, red, orange, gold, a white-hot center. The closest to the real thing. Risk: the white top can read as a hole in the heat on parchment, as Snowfall's pale top once did.

B: Sun, deep. The same path held a step darker: more red at the edge, ending at bright gold instead of white. Less literal, but it keeps the center solid and stands out more against the gold-brown map.

What to look for. Parchment is itself orange-brown, so a warm-only ramp has less contrast than the others. Check that the middle of each glow does not melt into the map.
