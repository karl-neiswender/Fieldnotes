Time counts only while you're logged in and not AFK, starting with this update. All shows each character's /played as well, so play from before the update is included there.

What got simpler

  • One control. Week, 2 Weeks and Month show a column a day (the last 7, 14 or 30 days); Year a column a month; All a column a year. No separate Day / Week / Month switch, and no arrows.
  • One headline instead of four tiles: time played in the range, how many characters, and against the range before it.
  • A click on a column narrows the page to that day (or month). "Back" or a second click returns to the range.
  • Gathered is one strip of totals, money last, instead of a card of bars.
  • When you play: the day by hour grid from Stats, filled with time played instead of gathers. It follows the range and a clicked day, and says when you play most.
  • By class and Your characters are one list. Each class is a group ("Hunter, 6h 10m"), each character a row with level, time and gold looted. Click a character for what they gathered. On All, the list is the full roster with /played.
  • The gathering-versus-everything-else bar is gone.

Review pass: more detail, still one page

  • Three facts under the headline: days played (13 of 14), a day you play on average, longest session. A clicked day swaps them for sessions, longest session and first log in.
  • A class key under the chart with each class's share. Before, the colors could only be read by hovering. Hover a class to pick it out of the columns.
  • A thin bar under each character, in class color, so you can see who got the time without reading the numbers.
  • Gathered totals show an hour played rate on hover (skins an hour, coin an hour).
  • Fixed: days counted twice across the clock change (it read "15 of 14"), and the hour grid ran wider than the dock.

AFK

Not counted. The switch under the headline adds it back for anyone who wants /played's way of counting. It moves every number on the page. The setting is remembered.

Gold

Each character's row shows the coin they looted in the range (Fieldnotes already records it). Opening a character also shows the gold they're carrying, saved at log out.
