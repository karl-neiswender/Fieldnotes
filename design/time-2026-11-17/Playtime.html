Time: an account summary of your play

Mockup for discussion (2026-11-17, third take). Total play time across every character, by day, week, month or year, stacked by class. Click a column (or use the arrows) to open that day or week below: who you played, for how long, and what they gathered. Open a class for its characters. Names and numbers are made up; the clock icon and typefaces are stand-ins.

What changed from the second take

  • Time played, not gathering time. Every minute you're logged in, on every character, whether you gather or not.
  • Stacked by class instead of by profession, with a Day view added (the last 14 days).
  • Click to dial in. A column opens that day, week, month or year right under the chart; the arrows step back and forward. Everything below the chart follows it.
  • Classes count characters. "2 Hunters", and opening it lists each one with level, time and what they gathered.
  • Gathered for the chosen period: each profession's count and money, with how much came in per hour played.
  • Your characters: the whole roster grouped by class, with level, all-time /played and their gathering professions.
  • Days played, Session length and Recent sessions are gone, to keep the page about the account.

What Fieldnotes would start saving

  • Each character's class, level and gathering professions, when it logs in.
  • Minutes online per character per day, counted while you're logged in (a small number per day, so years stay small).
  • Each character's /played total, asked quietly at log in so the line doesn't show in chat.

Gathered numbers come from the records Fieldnotes already keeps. All of it stays on your computer and never goes into a share.

The top split

The bar under the period's time shows how much of it went to gathering (lighter) against everything else, from the gathers themselves, the same way the Stats page counts active time.

For you to decide

1. Should AFK time count? /played counts it; leaving it out shows time you were actually playing.
2. Day view: last 14 days, or the last 30?
3. Anything missing from the account summary (for example gold earned per character)?
