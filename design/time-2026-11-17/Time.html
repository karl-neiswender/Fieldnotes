What changed from the first take

  • Time is its own page: a Time tab with a clock sits right above Stats on the rail. Stats goes back to being about gathers, as it is today.
  • No "Unknown class": only the real classes are listed.
  • No profession chips. Plain dots read as classes on this page, and icons would repeat the rail's at a tiny size. The chart's legend is the filter instead: click a name to show only that profession, click it again for all.

The page, top to bottom

  1. Overview: time gathering, sessions, average session, longest session.
  2. By week, month or year: columns stacked by profession. Hover for time, sessions, gathers, pace and each profession's share. Under it: this period so far, the last one, and the average.
  3. Days played: a square for each of the last 35 days, lit on days you gathered. Your current streak and your best.
  4. By class: a bar per class in the game's class colors, longest first. Click a class for its characters.
  5. Session length: how many sessions were under 15 minutes, 15 to 30, 30 to 60, 1 to 2 hours, and longer.
  6. Recent sessions: the last five, with day, character, length and a thin bar of what you gathered.

Controls

Show and When work as on Stats. Picking a profession in the legend narrows the whole page to its time, and the subtitle under "Time" says which (try Skinning). The other names dim but stay clickable. With Show: Me, By class is left out.

About classes

Fieldnotes only saves names today, and the game can only tell an add-on the class of the character you're logged in as. So each character's class is saved when it logs in. A character you haven't logged into since the update can't be placed in a class yet, so it's simply left out of By class until you do. It still counts everywhere else on the page.
