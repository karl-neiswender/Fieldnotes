## Motion: switching Fieldnotes off and on (full-screen map), from Karl's canvas comment

- Off: the toolbar slides closed toward its own centre, which is the centre of the map
  window, until only the Fieldnotes name and switch remain. At the same time the key, pins,
  badges and heat fade out while drifting a few pixels down.
- On: the reverse. The toolbar slides open outward from the centre, then the other pieces
  fade in and rise into place, a beat behind.
- Smooth and fluid: about 0.3 s, easing out as it opens and in as it closes. A second click
  mid-way reverses from where it is, never snaps. One driver while moving, stopped when done.
- The small-map dock does the same thing sideways: it slides in and out from the map edge.

## Motion: the settings sheet (small-map dock), from Karl's canvas comment

- The gear opens Map settings as a sheet that slides up from the bottom over the dock (about
  0.3 s, easing out), its content fading in. The dock's own content fades out and drifts down
  beneath it. Closing reverses it.
