Plasma: a fourth heat ramp

Sampled from a jet stream wind map: deep navy, royal blue, electric violet, magenta, hot pink, then a quick climb through coral and gold to a near-white core. Shown beside the three ramps we ship, on a parchment stand-in and on a darkened map, using the same fade as Map.lua (alpha 0.02 + 0.6 × v, quiet edge).

On the map
Map dimmed (night)

What the style is called. A perceptual sequential colormap (the plasma / inferno family that wind and radar maps use), drawn as a glow: color and opacity rise together, so the busiest core blooms toward white.

How it differs from Solar. Solar starts at a muted indigo and tops out at butter yellow. Plasma starts saturated blue, spends longer in violet and pink, and ends at a white hot core. It is the most "electric" of the four.

Dark matters. The wind map glows because it sits on near black. On parchment the low blues still read, but the bloom is strongest on the dimmed map, so a light dim under the heat when Plasma is chosen is worth trying.
