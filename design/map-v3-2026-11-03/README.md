Map third pass: four ideas taken from other heat map work (a city sensor dashboard Karl shared,
2026-11-03). Open Dock.html in a browser. Approved by Karl 2026-11-03 and built (Map.lua, MapUI.lua).

1. Hot spots list in the dock: the three spots Map.lua already pins (rank, skins, creatures,
   coordinates). Row and pin light together on hover; a click pings the spot. Pins show their rank.
2. Quiet areas fade out: the bottom of the Thermal ramp fades to clear instead of tinting the
   whole zone blue. Colors unchanged. The page's "Quiet areas" switch compares Faded and As now.
3. A key with numbers: counts under the ramp (scaled to the map's densest spot) replace Fewer/More.
4. Every skin over the heat: a small switch beside View lays leather-coloured dots over Heat or
   Spotlight.

The full-screen toolbar gets 2 and 3; its hot spots stay as pins with tooltips.
