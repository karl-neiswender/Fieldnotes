Fieldnotes ‹
  1. Hot spots listThe three spots the map already pins, now named in the dock: rank, skins, the creatures there and map coordinates. Hover a row and its pin rings in gold; hover a pin and its row lights. Click a row to ping the spot. The pins carry their rank number.
  2. Quiet areas fade outThe coolest part of the Thermal ramp fades to clear, so the blue haze over the whole zone goes and the hot spots stand out. Thermal's colors are unchanged. Use the switch above to compare.
  3. A key with numbersFewer and More become real counts under the ramp, scaled to this map. The ramp's left end fades the same way the map does.
  4. Every skin, over the heatA small switch beside View lays one dot per skin over Heat or Spotlight (Points already shows them). Dots take their leather colors, smaller and fainter than in Points.

Kept as is: colors, layout, the panel look. The full-screen toolbar gets the same fade and key numbers; its hot spots stay as pins with tooltips (no room for a list).
