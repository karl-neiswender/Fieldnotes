1. A tab for each profession

The Fieldnotes tab stays on top and opens the dashboard. Under it, one small tab per profession, in that profession's color. Only professions this character knows get a tab, plus Cloth for everyone. In Account view, any profession with notes shows.

A green dot on a tab means this session is recording it. Click Fieldnotes again on any page to fold the dock, as now.

2. The dashboard

One row per profession: count for the When choice, the zone you gather it most, skill and cap. Click a row to open its tab. On the map, every gather is a dot in its profession's color, no heat (adding five professions' heat together means nothing). View and the key belong to the profession tabs.

3. One session for everything

One clock. A row appears the first time you gather something new this session (try the buttons under the tracker). Each row: count, per hour on the shared clock, skill +N, and the cap note when it applies. Loot is the session's top items across all of them.

Folded, it is one line: clock, then a colored dot and count per profession.

4. Colors

Identity colors only: icon, tab edge, dots, rows. The heat ramps (Thermal, Solar, Snowfall) stay as they are on the profession tabs.

Pure yellow mining sits close to the gold chosen state and next to orange skinning. Sand keeps it yellow but apart. Compare with the switch at the top right.
