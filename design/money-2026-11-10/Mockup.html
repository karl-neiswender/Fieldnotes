Money: a new category

Mockup for discussion (2026-11-10). Two decisions: what counts as money, and which color it gets. Typefaces and icons are stand-ins. Sample session: 31 minutes in Stranglethorn Vale.

1. What counts as money?

ACoin you loot suggested

The copper, silver and gold in a loot window, from creatures and chests.

  • Has a place, so it gets a map tab and heat like every other category: where the money drops.
  • Exact. Fieldnotes already sees coin in loot windows.
  • Leaves out what your leather and ore are worth.

BPlus "worth at a vendor" suggested, as a second line

Each item you gathered, priced at what a vendor pays. Shown in the session, never on the map.

  • Answers "was this farm worth it?" without selling anything.
  • The game knows every item's vendor price.
  • An estimate. The auction house pays more for most leather and herbs.

CWhat you actually sell

Vendor sales (the money that arrives while a vendor is open) and auction house sales (read from your mail).

  • The real number.
  • Not gathering: it happens in town, often days later, and has no place on the map.
  • AH money only shows when you open your mail. Belongs in a later ledger feature, if at all.

Suggestion: A is the category (tab, map, session row). B is one extra line under it in the session window and the stats. C waits.

2. Which color?

The same dock tabs and session window in both. Money is chosen on the tabs. In the session, Pause is the gold "next step" button and the loot counts are gold.

AKeep today's gold look

Gold stays the chosen state, the headings and the window edge (Forever Goal Tracker's look). Money gets a coin yellow. Money's numbers, the chosen tab and the loot counts all read as the same gold, so it is hard to tell "money" from "selected".
