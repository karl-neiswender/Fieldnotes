Fieldnotes stats v5: stats inside the dock, as a phone-style page of charts

The Stats tab stops opening a separate window. The dock grows a little wider and the page scrolls top to bottom: headline numbers, then one chart per question. Every chart answers on hover with a game-style tooltip. Pick a profession with the chips; "All" compares them. Data here is made up, shaped like the demo.

What changes

  1. No stats window. The Stats tab (bar-chart icon) opens a page inside the dock, like Settings does. The old window and /fn stats go.
  2. The dock is wider: 228 to 272 (about a fifth). The map's own size is untouched; the toolbar on the full-screen map keeps its key.
  3. One vertical page that scrolls with the mouse wheel: numbers first, then charts, each answering one question.
  4. Chips pick All or one profession. A profession adds its own card at the bottom (Skinning: ruined rate and creatures; Cloth: drop rate by creature; Treasure: best chests).
  5. Hover anything for a tooltip in the game's own style. Hovering a donut slice or legend row lights both.

The charts

  • Last 30 days: a column a day, stacked by profession.
  • Profession mix: a donut with the total in the middle.
  • When you gather: day by hour, darker where you gather most.
  • Top loot: bars with the item's own icon; hover for per-gather rate.
  • Best zones: gathers per hour of active time, from sessions in the records.
  • Skill climb: a line per skilled profession over the period.

How it is built in game

Bars, columns and the grid are plain textures, pooled and redrawn only when the page, filter or data changes. The donut is a ring cut into 90 thin wedges (one rotated texture each), colored by share. Lines are rotated 2px textures between points. Text is the game's fonts; the tooltip is GameTooltip.

Not a phone UI: no swiping. The wheel scrolls, the chips filter, hover explains.
