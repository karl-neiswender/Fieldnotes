Stats v5 (2026-11-05): stats move into the dock. Open Dock.html in a browser. Awaiting Karl's review.

Karl's brief: instead of a separate window, make the dock slightly wider and lay the stats out
vertically, like a phone. More charts (bars, pies, interactive hover tooltips), "like Infogram,
but WoW gathering data".

1. The Stats rail tab opens a page inside the dock (as Settings does). The stats window goes.
2. The dock widens from 228 to 272.
3. One scrolling page: Account/When dropdowns, chips (All or one profession), four headline
   numbers, then Last 30 days (stacked columns), Profession mix or What it gave (donut),
   When you gather (day x hour grid), Top loot (bars with item icons), Best zones (per hour),
   Skill climb (lines, with rank caps), and a card of the chosen profession's own.
4. Every bar, slice, square and point shows a GameTooltip on hover; a donut slice and its legend
   row light together.

In game: pooled textures, redrawn only on page, filter or data change. Donut: a ring of 90
rotated wedge textures. Lines: rotated 2px textures. Game fonts only.

Also shown (already built): softer tab wash (LIGHT_PEAK 0.34 to 0.24) and a gold edge on the
open tab, full on its outer side and fading along the top and bottom, one gold for all tabs.
