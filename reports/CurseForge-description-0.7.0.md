## Description

# Fieldnotes

A gathering journal for **Classic Era** and **Warcraft Forever**. Fieldnotes remembers every creature you skin, every vein you mine, every herb you pick, every fish you catch and every cloth drop: what it gave you and exactly where you stood. Then it draws it all on your world map, so the best grounds are easy to find.

## Features

- **Heat map on your world map:** a soft glow where you gather most, numbered hot spots, a key with real counts and three color schemes. Or switch to **Points** (one dot per gather) or **Spotlight** (the map dims except where you gather).
- **Five professions:** Skinning, Mining, Herbalism, Fishing and Cloth, each on its own tab beside the map with its own material list and hot spots.
- **Home dashboard:** every profession at a glance, with counts, best zones and skill. Every gather shows on the map in its profession's color; hover a profession to light its spots.
- **Cloth drop rates:** every humanoid you loot is counted, cloth or not, so you know how often an area really drops cloth.
- **Me, Account or Community:** your character, all your characters, or notes other players have shared.
- **Session tracker:** one small window, one clock for everything you gather: count, per hour, skill gained, and a heads-up when it's time for a trainer.
- **Share your notes** as one line of text for Discord or a friend's Import box. No character names, realms or times of day are ever shared.
- **Try it first:** `/fn demo` loads sample notes for every profession.

## Getting started

Open your world map. Fieldnotes sits in a dock on its edge; the **Fieldnotes** tab folds it away. Then go gathering: your notes start with your first skin, vein, herb, catch or loot.

`/fn` stats window, `/fn demo` sample notes (`/fn demo off` removes them), `/fn share`, `/fn import`, `/fn tracker`, `/fn chat`.
