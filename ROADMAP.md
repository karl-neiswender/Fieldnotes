# Fieldnotes roadmap

What's coming next, roughly in order. Nothing here has a fixed date, and the order can change as
Warcraft Forever launches and as players say what they want most.

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/karl-neiswender/Fieldnotes/issues).

## Shipped

- **0.1 to 0.6:** skinning records, the world map heat map with Points and Spotlight, hot spots,
  Me / Account / Community, continent badges, the stats window, the session tracker, sharing and
  importing notes, and the map dock.
- **0.7:** Mining, Herbalism, Fishing and Cloth; a tab for each profession and a Home dashboard; one
  session for every profession; Stats and Settings tabs; a new look.

## Next

- **Warcraft Forever checks** after its launch: gathering, loot and map positions on the live client.
- **Stats and Share for every profession:** the stats window and share text still cover skinning only.
- **A profession switch on the full-screen map,** to match the dock's tabs.
- **Node names everywhere:** veins and herbs by name in hot spots and tooltips.

## Later

- **Community dataset:** shared notes merged and shipped with releases, so new players start with a
  map full of known grounds.
- **When you gather:** a day and hour grid, to see when spots are busiest.
- **Compare views:** your notes against the community's.
